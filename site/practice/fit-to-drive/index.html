Quiz: Fit to drive and paying attention

7 practice questions written by this site, not official DVSA questions. Choose one answer for each, then check. Each explanation names the Highway Code rule it comes from. Your result is saved only in this browser, if you allow it.

  1. Question 1 of 7 You feel sleepy before a long motorway journey. What is the best decision?

    The answer is: Do not start until you have rested

    Tiredness greatly raises your collision risk, and the Code says not to begin a journey when you are tired.

    Highway Code: Rule 91

  2. Question 2 of 7 You wear glasses to read a number plate at 20 metres. When must you wear them to drive?

    The answer is: All the time you are driving

    If you need glasses or contact lenses to meet the 20 metre standard, you must wear them at all times while driving.

    Highway Code: Rule 92

  3. Question 3 of 7 The low sun is dazzling you. What should you do?

    The answer is: Slow down, and stop if you need to

    Rule 93 says to slow down and, if necessary, stop when dazzled.

    Highway Code: Rule 93

  4. Question 4 of 7 You take a medicine and the leaflet says it may make you drowsy. What should you do?

    The answer is: Ask a doctor or pharmacist, and do not drive if you may be impaired

    You must not drive under the influence of drugs or medicine. Check advice and do not drive if you may be impaired.

    Highway Code: Rule 96

  5. Question 5 of 7 Your phone rings while you are stopped at red traffic lights, engine running. May you pick it up?

    The answer is: No, the ban applies even when stationary in traffic

    You must not pick up and use a hand-held phone even while stationary in traffic.

    Highway Code: Rule 149

  6. Question 6 of 7 Which of these can distract you and should be avoided while driving?

    The answer is: Arguing with a passenger

    Arguing, loud music, reading maps and adjusting the radio are listed as distractions.

    Highway Code: Rule 148

  7. Question 7 of 7 You have a health condition that is likely to affect your driving. What must you do?

    The answer is: Report it to the DVLA

    You must report to the DVLA any health condition likely to affect your driving.

    Highway Code: Rule 90
