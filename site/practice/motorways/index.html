Quiz: Motorways

9 practice questions written by this site, not official DVSA questions. Choose one answer for each, then check. Each explanation names the Highway Code rule it comes from. Your result is saved only in this browser, if you allow it.

  1. Question 1 of 9 Which of these must not use a motorway?

    The answer is: A cyclist

    Cyclists, pedestrians and horse riders, among others, must not use motorways.

    Highway Code: Rule 253

  2. Question 2 of 9 You join a motorway from a slip road. Who has priority?

    The answer is: Traffic already on the motorway

    Give priority to traffic already on the motorway, and match your speed to fit into the flow.

    Highway Code: Rule 259

  3. Question 3 of 9 You have missed your exit on the motorway. What should you do?

    The answer is: Carry on to the next exit

    You must not reverse, cross the central reservation or drive against the flow. Carry on to the next exit.

    Highway Code: Rule 263

  4. Question 4 of 9 Which lane should you normally drive in on a motorway?

    The answer is: The left-hand lane unless overtaking

    Keep in the left lane unless overtaking, and return to it when it is safe.

    Highway Code: Rule 264

  5. Question 5 of 9 On which side should you overtake on a motorway?

    The answer is: The right

    Overtake only on the right, and do not overtake on the left except in the limited congested conditions the Code describes.

    Highway Code: Rule 267, Rule 268

  6. Question 6 of 9 When may you use the hard shoulder?

    The answer is: In an emergency or when directed by police, traffic officers or a sign

    You must not use the hard shoulder except in an emergency or when directed to.

    Highway Code: Rule 269, Rule 271

  7. Question 7 of 9 You see a red flashing signal and a red X above your lane. What must you do?

    The answer is: Not drive in that lane, and move safely to an open lane

    A red X or flashing red lights identify a closed lane which you must not drive in.

    Highway Code: Rule 258

  8. Question 8 of 9 You leave a motorway after a long drive. Why check your speedometer?

    The answer is: Your speed may be higher than it feels, as 50 mph can feel like 30 mph

    After a spell at higher speed, the Code warns that 50 mph may feel like 30 mph.

    Highway Code: Rule 274

  9. Question 9 of 9 What does a flashing amber signal on a motorway tell you?

    The answer is: A hazard ahead, so slow down and be prepared

    Flashing amber signals warn of a hazard ahead. Reduce speed and be prepared.

    Highway Code: Rule 257
