Quiz: Lanes, overtaking and being overtaken

8 practice questions written by this site, not official DVSA questions. Choose one answer for each, then check. Each explanation names the Highway Code rule it comes from. Your result is saved only in this browser, if you allow it.

  1. Question 1 of 8 On a two-lane dual carriageway where should you normally drive?

    The answer is: The left-hand lane

    Stay in the left lane and use the right lane to overtake or turn right.

    Highway Code: Rule 137

  2. Question 2 of 8 Which of these places is it wrong to overtake?

    The answer is: On the approach to a bend you cannot see round

    Do not overtake where you cannot see far enough ahead, such as approaching a corner, hump bridge or the brow of a hill.

    Highway Code: Rule 166

  3. Question 3 of 8 You want to overtake a long lorry. What should you do first?

    The answer is: Drop back to see better and be seen

    Dropping back lets you see further ahead and lets the driver see you in their mirrors.

    Highway Code: Rule 164

  4. Question 4 of 8 As a guide, how much room should you leave when overtaking a cyclist at up to 30 mph?

    The answer is: At least 1.5 metres

    Rule 163 gives at least 1.5 metres as a guide at speeds up to 30 mph, and more at higher speeds.

    Highway Code: Rule 163

  5. Question 5 of 8 Someone is overtaking you. What should you do?

    The answer is: Keep a steady course and speed, slowing down if necessary

    Never obstruct drivers who want to pass. Speeding up or driving unpredictably is dangerous.

    Highway Code: Rule 168

  6. Question 6 of 8 A solid white line marks a cycle lane. May you drive in it while it is operating?

    The answer is: No, you must not

    You must not drive or park in a cycle lane marked by a solid white line during its times of operation.

    Highway Code: Rule 140

  7. Question 7 of 8 Which of these is a place where you should not overtake?

    The answer is: Approaching a junction

    Do not overtake where you may come into conflict with others, including at or approaching a road junction.

    Highway Code: Rule 167

  8. Question 8 of 8 You are being held up by a slow vehicle but there is a double white line with the solid line on your side. What do you do?

    The answer is: Do not overtake, with limited exceptions

    You must not overtake if it would mean crossing or straddling double white lines with the solid line nearest you.

    Highway Code: Rule 165
