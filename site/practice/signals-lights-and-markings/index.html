Quiz: Signals, lights, the horn and road markings

8 practice questions written by this site, not official DVSA questions. Choose one answer for each, then check. Each explanation names the Highway Code rule it comes from. Your result is saved only in this browser, if you allow it.

  1. Question 1 of 8 Another driver flashes their headlights at a junction. What should you assume?

    The answer is: That they are telling you they are there, so decide for yourself

    Never assume flashing headlights are an invitation to proceed. Use your own judgement.

    Highway Code: Rule 110, Rule 111

  2. Question 2 of 8 When may you use your horn?

    The answer is: While moving, to warn others of your presence

    Use the horn only while moving and only to warn of your presence, never aggressively.

    Highway Code: Rule 112

  3. Question 3 of 8 When may you use front or rear fog lights?

    The answer is: Only when visibility is seriously reduced

    Fog lights dazzle others and can hide your brake lights, so use them only when visibility is seriously reduced.

    Highway Code: Rule 114, Rule 236

  4. Question 4 of 8 Are you allowed to drive with hazard warning lights flashing on a single carriageway to warn of a queue behind you?

    The answer is: No, only on a motorway or unrestricted dual carriageway to warn of a hazard ahead

    You must not use hazard lights while driving unless on a motorway or unrestricted dual carriageway to warn drivers behind of a hazard or obstruction ahead.

    Highway Code: Rule 116

  5. Question 5 of 8 The centre white line lengthens and the gaps between the dashes get shorter. What does this tell you?

    The answer is: There is a hazard ahead

    A lengthening centre line with shortening gaps warns of a hazard ahead.

    Highway Code: Rule 127

  6. Question 6 of 8 On a road with double white lines, the solid line is nearest to you. What does this mean?

    The answer is: You must not cross or straddle it, with limited exceptions

    With the solid line nearest you, you must not cross or straddle it except in the limited cases the rule lists.

    Highway Code: Rule 129

  7. Question 7 of 8 What colour road studs mark the left-hand edge of the carriageway?

    The answer is: Red

    Red studs mark the left edge, white the lanes or centre, amber the central reservation, green lay-bys and slip roads.

    Highway Code: Rule 132

  8. Question 8 of 8 A police officer signals you to stop. What must you do?

    The answer is: Obey the signal

    You must obey signals given by police officers.

    Highway Code: Rule 105
