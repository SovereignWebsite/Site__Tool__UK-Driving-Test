Quiz: Before you set off

6 practice questions written by this site, not official DVSA questions. Choose one answer for each, then check. Each explanation names the Highway Code rule it comes from. Your result is saved only in this browser, if you allow it.

  1. Question 1 of 6 Who is responsible for making sure a 10-year-old child wears a seat belt or suitable restraint in your car?

    The answer is: The driver

    The driver must make sure children under 14 use a seat belt or approved restraint where required.

    Highway Code: Rule 100

  2. Question 2 of 6 A child is 1.2 metres tall. Which is correct?

    The answer is: They need a child seat, booster seat or booster cushion suited to their weight

    A child under 1.35 metres tall must use a baby seat, child seat, booster seat or booster cushion suitable for their weight.

    Highway Code: Rule 100

  3. Question 3 of 6 Where must you never fit a rear-facing baby seat?

    The answer is: In a seat protected by an active front airbag

    In a crash an active airbag can seriously injure or kill a child in a rear-facing seat.

    Highway Code: Rule 101

  4. Question 4 of 6 Before pulling away from the kerb, what should you do after checking your mirrors?

    The answer is: Look round for blind spots, signal if needed, then take a final look

    Mirrors, blind spots, signal if necessary, then a final check round before moving off.

    Highway Code: Rule 159

  5. Question 5 of 6 Before driving a vehicle, which of these must you have?

    The answer is: A valid licence and insurance for that vehicle

    You must have a valid licence and insurance, and the vehicle must be legal and roadworthy.

    Highway Code: Rule 97

  6. Question 6 of 6 Which door should children use to get out of the car where possible?

    The answer is: The one nearest the kerb

    Children should get in and out through the door nearest the kerb.

    Highway Code: Rule 102
