Quiz: Vulnerable road users and large vehicles

8 practice questions written by this site, not official DVSA questions. Choose one answer for each, then check. Each explanation names the Highway Code rule it comes from. Your result is saved only in this browser, if you allow it.

  1. Question 1 of 8 Which road users carry the greatest responsibility to reduce danger to others?

    The answer is: Those in charge of vehicles that can cause the most harm

    The hierarchy of road users says those who can cause the greatest harm bear the greatest responsibility, though everyone must take care.

    Highway Code: Rule H1

  2. Question 2 of 8 You see a school crossing patrol holding up a ‘Stop for children’ sign. What must you do?

    The answer is: Stop

    You must stop when a school crossing patrol shows a ‘Stop for children’ sign.

    Highway Code: Rule 210

  3. Question 3 of 8 Why should you slow down near schools?

    The answer is: Because children and young cyclists may appear suddenly

    Near schools drive slowly and be aware of young cyclists and pedestrians.

    Highway Code: Rule 208, Rule 205

  4. Question 4 of 8 Why might a cyclist ride in the middle of a lane on a narrow road?

    The answer is: To be seen and stay safe, so you should allow it

    On narrow roads and in slow traffic cyclists may ride in the centre of the lane, which can be safer for them.

    Highway Code: Rule 213

  5. Question 5 of 8 You are approaching a horse and rider. What should you do?

    The answer is: Pass wide and slowly

    Pass horse riders and horse-drawn vehicles wide and slowly.

    Highway Code: Rule 215

  6. Question 6 of 8 Why are motorcyclists and cyclists easy to miss at junctions?

    The answer is: They are smaller and hard to see, especially when alongside or coming up from behind

    The Code notes they are often difficult to see, especially at junctions and when overtaking or filtering through traffic.

    Highway Code: Rule 211

  7. Question 7 of 8 An ambulance with flashing blue lights approaches from behind. What should you do?

    The answer is: Think about its route and let it pass safely, still obeying traffic signs

    Do not panic. Take appropriate action to let it pass, avoiding stopping before a bend, brow or narrow section.

    Highway Code: Rule 219

  8. Question 8 of 8 You are following a large lorry and cannot see its mirrors. What does this suggest?

    The answer is: The driver may not be able to see you

    If you cannot see the driver in their mirrors, they cannot see you. Large vehicles also need extra room to turn.

    Highway Code: Rule 221
