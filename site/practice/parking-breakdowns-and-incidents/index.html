Quiz: Parking, breakdowns, incidents, level crossings and road works

8 practice questions written by this site, not official DVSA questions. Choose one answer for each, then check. Each explanation names the Highway Code rule it comes from. Your result is saved only in this browser, if you allow it.

  1. Question 1 of 8 What do double yellow lines at the edge of the road mean?

    The answer is: No waiting at any time

    Double yellow lines mean a prohibition of waiting at any time, even with no upright signs.

    Highway Code: Rule 238

  2. Question 2 of 8 Where must you not park?

    The answer is: At a pedestrian crossing or its zig-zag lines

    You must not stop or park on a crossing or in the area covered by its zig-zag lines.

    Highway Code: Rule 240, Rule 191

  3. Question 3 of 8 You park facing uphill on a hill. How should you leave the car?

    The answer is: In a forward gear with wheels turned away from the kerb

    Facing uphill: forward gear and steer away from the kerb. Facing downhill: reverse and steer towards the kerb.

    Highway Code: Rule 252

  4. Question 4 of 8 You break down on a motorway and cannot reach an exit. What is the best action?

    The answer is: Move left, stop as far left as possible, switch on hazard lights, and leave by the side away from traffic if it is safe

    Go left, stop as far left as possible, hazard lights on, and exit the vehicle by the side furthest from traffic if safe.

    Highway Code: Rule 277

  5. Question 5 of 8 You are involved in a collision that damages another person’s car and you do not give your details at the time. What must you do?

    The answer is: Report it to the police as soon as reasonably practicable and in any case within 24 hours

    If you do not give your details at the time, you must report to the police within 24 hours at the most.

    Highway Code: Rule 286

  6. Question 6 of 8 At a level crossing the red lights are flashing and the barriers are down. What must you do?

    The answer is: Stop behind the white line and wait

    You must always obey flashing red stop lights and stop behind the white line.

    Highway Code: Rule 293

  7. Question 7 of 8 You break down on a level crossing. What should you do first?

    The answer is: Get everyone out and clear of the crossing immediately

    Get everyone out and clear immediately, and use the railway telephone if there is one.

    Highway Code: Rule 299

  8. Question 8 of 8 You come across a road traffic collision with injured people. What should you do about those in a vehicle?

    The answer is: Not move them unless they are in immediate danger

    Do not move injured people from their vehicles unless they are in immediate danger, and call 999.

    Highway Code: Rule 283
