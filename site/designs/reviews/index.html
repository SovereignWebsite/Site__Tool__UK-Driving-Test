Design review log

What was checked in each round of design review, who did it, what was found and what changed. Rounds are added as they happen.

Round 1: keeper review

Reviewer: uk-driving-test.a (the site keeper): axe-core on all 48 pages, light and dark, at 1280 and 375 pixels wide, with a quiz checked; a scripted keyboard and storage test; screenshots of every design at both widths. Date: 25 November 2026. Status: done; round 2 (design.b, asked through a.maestro) is awaited.

Findings

  • Axe: the unofficial notice sat outside any landmark on every page (rule "region").
  • Axe: the Sovereign Website credit was low contrast on Quiz first (over the top bar) and on Road map (a dark page with a light-theme credit).
  • The quiz works with the keyboard alone, moves focus to the result, shows each answer with its rule, and with scripts off shows every answer and explanation.
  • No cookies, no external requests, and "clear my saved results" empties local storage.
  • Course path on a phone: the route takes a lot of the first screen.
  • All five designs are visibly different in structure: top bar, side route, bottom tabs, contents margin, road with stops.

Changes made

  • The notice is now an aside landmark with a label.
  • The credit colours are set for the dark page on Road map, and the override on Quiz first is removed.
  • Course path on a phone: the route sits in two columns at a smaller size.

Round 2: design.b review

Reviewer: design.b, requested through a.maestro. Review: https://design.agents.sovereignweb.site/reviews-uk-driving-test-2026-11-25-round-2.html (design.b reviewed from source, because the live address had no valid certificate yet; its synthetic-user run follows once the site serves). Date: 25 November 2026. Status: done; changes made; round 3 when the site is live.

Findings

  • design.b recommends Quiz first as the home and Road map as the showpiece, and disagrees with the keeper’s Course path: on a phone its first screen is a menu, and nothing says you are here.
  • The site title appeared twice on every first screen and the notice band took two lines.
  • No design showed progress, although results are already saved in the browser.
  • No imagery: the world of the theory test has strong pictures of its own.
  • Course path on a phone: collapse the route to one line. Highway Code book: nothing may read as the official Code.
  • Rated M2 until the synthetic-user run.

Changes made

  • The home heading is now “Get ready for the UK theory test”, so the title shows once.
  • The notice is one line on a phone: “Unofficial: not the DVSA or GOV.UK”.
  • Progress from the learner’s own saved results: best scores on the Quiz first tiles, the Road map stop for practice fills in, and Course path says “You are here”. The book keeps its contents list.
  • Signs drawn by this site from basic shapes (triangle, circle, rectangle), not copied from the Highway Code: on the Road map stops, on the Quiz first start card and on the Highway Code book plate. Plain page has none, by design.
  • Course path on a phone: the route is one line, “Step 1 of 5: How the test works”, that opens on tap. On a desktop the route’s line is a dashed road marking.
  • The recommendation is now Quiz first. The Highway Code book still says “An unofficial study companion” under its title, and the notice is on every page.

Round 3: a.design review

Reviewer: a.design, account A’s design reviewer. Review: https://design.agents.sovereignweb.site/factory/dinis-cruz/account-a/a.design/reviews/uk-driving-test-2026-11-25-round-3.html (reviewed from source at 249fc62). Date: 25 November 2026. Status: done; changes made in round 4.

Findings

  • Axe clean on all 64 page, width and theme combinations; round 2 fully acted on. Recommends Quiz first as home and Road map as the showpiece.
  • The gap: only the home pages differ. Topic and quiz pages are the same long column in every design.
  • Quiz first should be one question per screen with progress, and the rule after each answer.
  • Quiz first in dark mode should keep its header and start card dark; it should have five tabs, not six.
  • Course path said “Four steps” and “Step 1 of 5” on one screen. The book showed its contents twice on a phone.
  • Road map stops need their state in words, and two stops left the design for the live pages. Plain page’s top links repeated the list below.

Changes made

  • Quiz first: one question per screen, a progress bar, a “Check answer” button, then the explanation and rule, then “Next question”. Without scripts it is still the full list with answers shown.
  • Quiz first: the header and start card stay dark in dark mode; five tabs, and About lives in the footer.
  • Course path: four steps everywhere (“Step 1 of 4”), with Sources and licence as a plain link beneath.
  • Highway Code book: no second contents list on its home page.
  • Road map: the practice stop says “Not started” or “Quiz results saved” in words. Each design now has its own How the test works and Hazard perception demonstration pages, so no stop leaves the design.
  • Plain page: no top links on its home page, where the list below does the job.
  • Not done yet: giving topic and quiz pages a different structure in each design. That is a larger piece of work, offered for round 5 if the owner wants it before choosing.
