Design directions

Choose a design. Five structural design directions for this site. They differ in layout and navigation, not only in colour. The live site uses one of them (Plain page) until the owner picks. Each demonstration has a home page, a Highway Code topic page and a working quiz, using the same content.

  • Plain page live now

    One calm column and a top bar. Text first, nothing to learn.

    Best for: Reading on any device, and the lowest maintenance.

    Home page A topic page A quiz

  • Course path

    A numbered route down the side. You always know the next step.

    Best for: Learners who like to be told what to do next.

    Home page A topic page A quiz

  • Quiz first recommended

    Practice is the front door. Big cards and a tab bar at the thumb.

    Best for: Phones, short sessions, learners who learn by testing.

    Home page A topic page A quiz

  • Highway Code book

    Like a well-set reference book: contents beside the text, rule numbers in the margin.

    Best for: Learners who study from the rules and want to see the source at once.

    Home page A topic page A quiz

  • Road map

    A journey down a road, with stops. High contrast, dark by default.

    Best for: Learners who like a sense of progress, and night-time revision.

    Home page A topic page A quiz

Recommendation

Quiz first. Quiz first, as home. After design.b’s round 2 the keeper changed its recommendation from Course path. The audience is first-time learners on phones, and what they come back to do is practise, so "Start a quiz" belongs on the first screen, with bottom tabs a phone user already knows. Course path (the runner-up, now with a one-line "Step 2 of 5" route on phones) is the better choice if the owner wants learners guided through the steps in order. Road map is the most memorable and suits being the showpiece for sharing. Plain page stays live until the owner picks.

The choice is the owner’s. See the review log for what was checked in each round and what changed.
