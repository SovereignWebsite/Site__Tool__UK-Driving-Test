Highway Code

Junctions, roundabouts and crossings

Where paths cross, priority matters. These rules cover who goes first, and how to approach.

  • Rule 171At a Stop sign with a solid white line you must stop behind the line, then wait for a safe gap.
  • Rule 172At a Give Way sign or broken white lines across the road, you must give way to traffic on the main road.
  • Rule 174Box junctions have yellow criss-cross lines. You must not enter until your exit is clear, though you may wait in the box to turn right if only oncoming traffic stops you.
  • Rule 175At traffic lights you must stop at the line unless the light is green. If amber appears, you may go on only if you have already crossed the line or are so close that stopping might cause a collision.
  • Rule 176You must not cross the line on red. On green, go only if there is room to clear the junction.
  • Rule 178At an advanced stop line for cyclists, motorists must stop at the first white line if the light is amber or red.
  • Rule 182Signal left in good time. Do not overtake just before turning left, and watch for traffic and cyclists on your left.
  • Rule 183When turning, give way to vehicles in bus lanes, cycle lanes, cycle tracks and tramways, from either direction.
  • Rule 184Approach a roundabout using all the information from signs, signals and lane markings, and use Mirrors, Signal, Manoeuvre.
  • Rule 185At a roundabout, give priority to traffic from your right unless signs, markings or lights say otherwise.
  • Rule 188At a mini-roundabout all vehicles must pass round the central marking, except large vehicles that physically cannot. Avoid U-turns there.
  • Rule 191You must not park on a crossing or on its zig-zag lines, and must not overtake the moving vehicle nearest the crossing, or one stopped to give way.
  • Rule 195At a zebra crossing, give way to pedestrians waiting, and you must give way once someone has stepped on. Do not wave people across, flash or sound your horn.
  • Rule 196At a pelican crossing you must stop on red. On flashing amber you must give way to anyone on the crossing, and may proceed with care if it is clear.
  • Rule 199Toucan, puffin and equestrian crossings follow the ordinary traffic light sequence, with no flashing amber phase.
  • Rule H2At a junction, give way to pedestrians crossing or waiting to cross the road you are turning into or out of.
  • Rule H3Do not cut across cyclists or horse riders going straight ahead when you turn. Wait for a safe gap.
