Quiz: Junctions, roundabouts and crossings

9 practice questions written by this site, not official DVSA questions. Choose one answer for each, then check. Each explanation names the Highway Code rule it comes from. Your result is saved only in this browser, if you allow it.

  1. Question 1 of 9 You come to a junction with a Stop sign and a solid white line. What must you do?

    The answer is: Stop behind the line

    You must stop behind the line, then move off only when there is a safe gap.

    Highway Code: Rule 171

  2. Question 2 of 9 The traffic light turns amber as you approach. When may you carry on?

    The answer is: Only if you have already crossed the line or are so close that stopping might cause a collision

    On amber you may go on only if you have crossed the stop line or stopping might cause a collision.

    Highway Code: Rule 175

  3. Question 3 of 9 When may you enter a box junction?

    The answer is: When your exit road or lane is clear, or you are waiting to turn right and only oncoming traffic stops you

    You must not enter until your exit is clear, except to wait to turn right when stopped only by oncoming traffic or others turning right.

    Highway Code: Rule 174

  4. Question 4 of 9 At a roundabout, who normally has priority?

    The answer is: Traffic from your right

    Give priority to traffic approaching from your right, unless signs, markings or lights say otherwise.

    Highway Code: Rule 185

  5. Question 5 of 9 A pedestrian has stepped onto a zebra crossing. What must you do?

    The answer is: Give way

    You must give way once a pedestrian has moved onto a zebra crossing. Do not wave, flash or sound your horn.

    Highway Code: Rule 195

  6. Question 6 of 9 At a pelican crossing the amber light is flashing and a pedestrian is still crossing. What must you do?

    The answer is: Give way to them

    When the amber light is flashing you must give way to any pedestrians on the crossing.

    Highway Code: Rule 196

  7. Question 7 of 9 You are turning left at a junction and a pedestrian is crossing the road you are turning into. What should you do?

    The answer is: Give way to them

    At a junction you should give way to pedestrians crossing or waiting to cross the road you are turning into.

    Highway Code: Rule H2

  8. Question 8 of 9 A cyclist is riding straight ahead past a junction you want to turn into. What should you do?

    The answer is: Wait for a safe gap and do not cut across them

    Do not turn if it would make a cyclist going ahead stop or swerve. Wait for a safe gap.

    Highway Code: Rule H3

  9. Question 9 of 9 You approach a mini-roundabout. Which is correct?

    The answer is: Pass round the central marking unless your vehicle is too large to

    All vehicles must pass round the central markings, except large vehicles physically unable to.

    Highway Code: Rule 188
