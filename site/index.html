UK driving theory test: learn and practise

Get ready for the UK theory test

Free, plain help for learning for the UK car theory test. Learn how the test works, read the Highway Code by topic, practise with questions that cite the rules, and understand hazard perception. Nothing to sign up for, and nothing tracks you.

  1. How the theory test works Two parts, the timings and pass marks, and what to take. From GOV.UK.
  2. The Highway Code by topic Ten topics, summarised, with every rule linked to the official text.
  3. Practice quizzes 85 questions in 11 topics, each citing its rule.
  4. Hazard perception explained What a developing hazard is and how the clips are scored.

This site is unofficial. The practice questions are written by this site and are not official DVSA questions. Always check GOV.UK for the rules, booking and fees. Sources and licence.
