Highway Code

Lanes, overtaking and being overtaken

Choosing a lane well and overtaking only when it is safe and legal, and helping others overtake you.

  • Rule 133To change lane: mirrors, a quick glance if needed, signal when it is safe, then move over when it is clear.
  • Rule 137On a two-lane dual carriageway stay in the left lane. Use the right-hand lane to overtake or turn right, then move back left when safe.
  • Rule 140You must not drive or park in a cycle lane marked by a solid white line during its hours. Avoid a broken-line cycle lane unless unavoidable.
  • Rule 141Unless signs say otherwise, do not drive in a bus lane while it is operating.
  • Rule 160Keep to the left unless you are overtaking, turning right or passing parked vehicles or pedestrians in the road.
  • Rule 162Before overtaking, make sure the road ahead is clear, nobody is starting to overtake you, and there is a gap to move into.
  • Rule 163Overtake only when safe and legal. When passing cyclists, as a guide leave at least 1.5 metres at speeds up to 30 mph, and more at higher speeds. For horse riders and horse-drawn vehicles, pass at under 10 mph and leave at least 2 metres.
  • Rule 164Drop back when you want to overtake a large vehicle. You see more and the driver can see you. It takes longer to pass one.
  • Rule 165You must not overtake if it would mean crossing double white lines with a solid line nearest you, entering a protected area bordered by a solid white line, or going past a ‘no overtaking’ sign, among other cases listed in the rule.
  • Rule 166Do not overtake if there is any doubt, or where you cannot see far enough ahead, for example approaching a bend, a hump bridge or the brow of a hill.
  • Rule 167Do not overtake where you might come into conflict with others, for example at or approaching a junction, where the road narrows, or near a school crossing patrol.
  • Rule 168When someone overtakes you, keep a steady course and speed, slowing if needed. Never speed up.
  • Rule 169Do not hold up a long queue. Pull in where it is safe and let traffic pass.
