The Highway Code by topic

The Highway Code, grouped into ten topics. Each topic gives a short summary in our own words, and links every rule to the full text on GOV.UK, which is the authority. Read the rules themselves as well as our summaries.

  1. 1Fit to drive and paying attentionTiredness, eyesight, alcohol, drugs, medicine, phones and other distractions.
  2. 2Before you set offRoadworthy vehicle, licence and insurance, seat belts, child restraints and moving off safely.
  3. 3Speed, space and stoppingSpeed limits, safe speed, braking, skids and the gap to keep.
  4. 4Signals, lights, the horn and road markingsSignals, flashing headlights, the horn, lights at night and in fog, hazard lights and white lines.
  5. 5Lanes, overtaking and being overtakenChanging lane, dual carriageways, special lanes, and when overtaking is and is not safe.
  6. 6Junctions, roundabouts and crossingsStop and give way, box junctions, traffic lights, turning, roundabouts and pedestrian crossings.
  7. 7Vulnerable road users and large vehiclesPedestrians, children, cyclists, motorcyclists, horse riders, learners, emergency vehicles and big vehicles.
  8. 8Driving in bad weatherRain, ice, snow, wind, fog and hot weather.
  9. 9MotorwaysJoining, lane discipline, overtaking, signals, the hard shoulder and leaving.
  10. 10Parking, breakdowns, incidents, level crossings and road worksWhere not to park, what to do when something goes wrong, railway crossings and road works.
