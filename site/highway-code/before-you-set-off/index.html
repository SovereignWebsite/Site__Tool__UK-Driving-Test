Highway Code

Before you set off

A short checklist, and the law behind it: the vehicle, the paperwork, the people in it, and the check around you before you move.

  • Rule 89Your vehicle and any trailer must meet the legal construction and lighting requirements.
  • Rule 97Before you set off you must have a valid licence and insurance for the vehicle, and the vehicle must be legal and roadworthy.
  • Rule 99You must wear a seat belt in a car, van or other goods vehicle where one is fitted. The rule has a table, and a few exemptions, for example for some medical reasons.
  • Rule 100The driver must make sure children under 14 wear a seat belt or sit in an approved child restraint where required. A child under 1.35 metres tall must use a child seat, booster seat or booster cushion suited to their weight.
  • Rule 101Never fit a rear-facing baby seat in a seat protected by an active front airbag.
  • Rule 102Check that child restraints are fitted to the maker’s instructions, and let children out of the car on the kerb side.
  • Rule 159Before moving off: check all mirrors, look round for blind spots, signal if it helps anyone, then take a final look round. Go only when it is safe.
