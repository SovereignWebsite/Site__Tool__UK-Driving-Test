Highway Code

Parking, breakdowns, incidents, level crossings and road works

The rules for when you stop, when something goes wrong, and when the road has changed.

  • Rule 238You must not wait or park on yellow lines during the times shown. Double yellow lines mean no waiting at any time, even without upright signs.
  • Rule 240You must not stop or park at a pedestrian crossing or its zig-zags, on a clearway, in a taxi bay, in a bus or cycle lane during its hours, and in other places the rule lists.
  • Rule 241Do not use parking spaces reserved for others, such as Blue Badge holders, unless you are entitled to.
  • Rule 242Do not leave your vehicle in a dangerous position or where it causes unnecessary obstruction.
  • Rule 243Do not stop or park near a school entrance, where it blocks emergency access, at or near bus stops, or on the approach to a level crossing, among other places listed.
  • Rule 252Parking on a hill: close to the kerb with the handbrake firmly on. Facing uphill, forward gear and wheels turned away from the kerb. Facing downhill, reverse gear and wheels turned towards the kerb.
  • Rule 275If you must stop after a breakdown or incident, try to stop in a place of relative safety, where you, your passengers and the vehicle are less at risk from moving traffic.
  • Rule 276If you break down, think of other road users first and get off the road if you can. Use hazard lights if you are causing an obstruction.
  • Rule 277On a motorway, leave at the next exit or service area if you can. If not, move left, stop as far left as possible, switch on hazard lights, and leave by the side away from traffic if it is safe.
  • Rule 283At an incident: stop in a place of relative safety, use hazard lights, ask for engines to be switched off, ring 999, and do not move injured people unless they are in immediate danger.
  • Rule 286If a collision causes damage or injury you must stop and give your details. If you do not do so at the time, report to the police as soon as reasonably practicable and in any case within 24 hours.
  • Rule 288When the ‘Road Works Ahead’ sign is displayed, take extra care and watch for further signs. Obey all signs and speed limits.
  • Rule 291Approach a level crossing with care. Never drive onto one until the road is clear on the far side.
  • Rule 293At controlled crossings you must always obey the flashing red stop lights and stop behind the line. Never zig-zag round half-barriers.
  • Rule 299If you break down on a level crossing, get everyone out and clear immediately and use the railway telephone if there is one.
