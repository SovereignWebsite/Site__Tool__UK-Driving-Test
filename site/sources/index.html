Sources and licence

Where everything on this site comes from, and what this site is not.

This site is unofficial

This site is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the DVSA (Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency) or GOV.UK. It carries no DVSA or GOV.UK branding. It is a free learning aid. For anything that matters, such as booking or fees, use GOV.UK.

What comes from where

  • How the theory test works: GOV.UK’s Theory test: cars pages. Each fact has its page linked and the date it was read: 24 November 2026.
  • The Highway Code summaries: written by this site in its own words from The Highway Code, with each rule linked to the official text.
  • Practice questions and explanations: written by this site from the Highway Code rules each cites. They are not official questions.

Licence and credit

The Highway Code is Crown copyright. It is used here under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

The DVSA’s official theory test revision question bank is separately licensed. This site has not copied it, and no question here is taken from it or modelled on its wording.

Privacy

This site uses no cookies, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party scripts. If you check a quiz, your best score for that topic can be kept in your own browser’s local storage. You can clear it with the button on the practice page.

Found a mistake?

Rules change. If something here disagrees with GOV.UK, GOV.UK is right. The source is in the public repository.

Not covered yet: the practical driving test, booking steps, and traffic signs.
